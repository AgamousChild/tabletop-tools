# 11th Edition Game Flow + Game-Tracker Requirements

> **Status:** Grounded research note. Synthesized entirely from locally-fetched YouTube
> auto-caption transcripts (Auspex Tactics, Tactical Tortoise, Play On Tabletop) covering
> Warhammer 40,000 11th-edition previews and GW live-stream battle reports, May–June 2026.
> This is **pre-launch information** — the full 11th-edition core rules were not released when
> these videos were recorded. Everything here reflects GW previews, Q&A answers, and live demo
> games, **not** a final rulebook. Auto-captions are noisy; low-confidence items are flagged in
> §6. Do not treat any number as final until verified against the published core rules.
>
> **Why this exists:** to ground an upcoming game-tracker redesign in *11th-edition* flow rather
> than carried-over 10th-edition assumptions. 10th-edition habits are exactly what we are avoiding.

## 1. Game Flow (11th Edition)

### 1.1 Pre-game

1. **Army / list construction.**
   - Game sizes unchanged in spirit: **Incursion (~1,000 pts), Strike Force (2,000 pts)**.
     GW guidance is that you use **Incursion rules all the way up to just under 2,000 points**,
     then Strike Force at 2,000+ ("HUGE Answers — Points, Army Support, Indirect Fire + More").
   - **Detachment points** — new army-construction system. You get **3 detachment points at
     2,000 pts**. Most detachments cost **2 points** (your main force), with **1-point "support"
     / add-on detachments** you bolt on, and a few powerful ones costing **3 points** (which then
     can't take an add-on). Typical build is a **2+1**. Units don't declare which detachment they
     belong to; they use the rules of all detachments that apply. ~70 new detachments at launch
     ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far"; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals" confirmed
     specific detachments as 2-point or 3-point).
   - **Enhancements:** scale with game size — **2 at 1,000 pts, 4 at 2,000 pts** (one more than
     10th). New **"upgrade enhancements"** can apply to characters *or* other units and can be
     taken on **up to 3 units**, paying the point cost each time but counting as **one** selection
     against your max ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far").
   - **Wargear / loadout points return** — but only for **some** big primary-weapon choices, not
     every bolt pistol. A unit's big weapon options can cost different points
     ("Wargear Points are Coming Back"; "HUGE Answers …").
   - **Datasheet rule of 3** unchanged: 3 of each datasheet at 2,000, 2 at Incursion.
   - **Leader / Support attachments declared on the army list.** A unit can have **one Leader
     character + one Support character** attached simultaneously (see §4)
     ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far").
   - **Force disposition:** each detachment has an associated **force disposition**; you pick your
     army's disposition from your available detachments, which determines what primary objectives
     you chase (asymmetric missions — see §3) ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far").

2. **Battlefield & terrain setup.** Terrain is placed on **terrain footprints**; **objective
   markers are now terrain features**, not circles. GW ships preset tournament-style layouts tied
   to specific deployment types. New **"solid" rule**: terrain below 3" in height blocks line of
   sight and is played as if windows don't exist (the old "ground-floor ruins are closed" house
   rule, now official) ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").

3. **Deployment.** Roll-off; loser may be made to deploy first / be defender (batreps show
   "you can be defender and deploy first"). Deployment types confirmed still include **Dawn of
   War, Hammer and Anvil, Search and Destroy (table quarters), Sweeping Engagement, Tipping
   Point** ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; batreps name Sweeping Engagement and Search and Destroy).
   Board objectives are named **home objective**, **central objective**, and **expansion
   objectives** (the two in no-man's-land) ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").

4. **Reserves / pre-game moves.** Units can start in **Strategic Reserve** / **Deep Strike**
   (arrive **>8"** from enemies — down from 9" in 10th). **Scout** moves and other pre-game moves
   happen before the first turn (batreps: Gretchin scout onto an objective; Tzaangors scout move).
   **Infiltrate and Scout no longer stack** — a unit with both must pick one at deployment. Scout
   gains a new option: a scouting unit can **start off the battlefield and deploy after all other
   models** (redeploy timing) instead of taking an early deployment slot
   ("EVERY Core Rules Update …" — deep strike is now 8"; "MASSIVE 11th Edition Rule Drops" for the
   infiltrate/scout change).

5. **Determine first turn.** Roll-off for who goes first (batreps: "It's a three. It's a six.
   You're going first."). Asymmetric primary missions and "go second" advantages (end-of-battle
   scoring, late objective grabs) make turn order matter ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").

### 1.2 The battle round

A game is **5 battle rounds**. Each battle round = **both players take one player-turn**
(the player who has first turn, then the other). Several things now happen **at the start of the
battle round** (before turns), e.g. mission/objective re-rolls and any escalation rules
(batreps: objectives are re-rolled/re-loaded "at the start of the battle round").

### 1.3 The player turn — phase sequence

Order is unchanged from 10th: **Command → Movement → Shooting → Charge → Fight → (end of turn).**
The changes are *within* the phases.

1. **Command phase**
   - **Gain Command Points.** Both players gain **1 CP** at the start of the battle round / turn
     (batreps: "we both get a command point"). Extra CP from rules (e.g. Gretchin farming a CP on
     a 4+) — see §2.
   - **Battle Shock tests.** A unit at **half strength or below** tests Battle Shock. **Battle
     Shock no longer auto-recovers** — a shocked unit must **test to regroup** in a later command
     phase ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far"; "MASSIVE 11th Edition Rule Drops").
     Battle Shock reduces **OC to "OC-dash"** (likely un-modifiable by +OC effects). Leader
     characters now test as part of their unit and keep testing when reduced to just the lone
     character ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals"). Batreps show a dedicated, often noisy, per-unit
     battle-shock step at the start of each turn.
   - **Score primary** (on missions that score at end of command phase — see §3) and trigger
     start-of-turn abilities (e.g. Crimson King aura, "sticky objective" strategems, doctrine
     selection) (all three Play On batreps).

2. **Movement phase**
   - Normal moves, Advance, Fall Back, **disembark**, **reinforcements arrive** (deep strike /
     strategic reserve at **>8"**), reactive moves.
   - **Engagement range is now 2"** (up from 1"). You can't *end* a move within 2" of enemies but
     can move *through* that zone mid-move ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far").
   - **FLY** must "take to the skies," paying **2" of movement**, in exchange for ignoring vertical
     distance and **moving horizontally/vertically through terrain as if it weren't there**.
     Big buff to bikes/grav-tanks/monsters; using fly in a charge applies **−2" to the charge**
     ("FLY Buffed, Knights, Emperor's Children + Grey Knights").
   - **Coherency change:** standard 2"-to-another-model coherency stays, but the old "2 models for
     big units" rule is replaced by **every model within 9" of every other model** in the unit
     ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").
   - **Fire Overwatch** now fires **at the *end* of the movement phase** (you see where enemies
     moved); cannot be used in the charge phase; can't trigger at the *start* of a move; resolved
     as **snap shots** (hit only on 6s, no hit roll, no modifiers)
     ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals"). Batreps show Overwatch fired in
     both movement and after charges declared — verify exact timing (§6).
   - **Vehicles may no longer need to pay for pivoting** — they can rotate freely
     (low confidence; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").

3. **Shooting phase**
   - **Benefit of Cover = −1 to Ballistic Skill** (a hit-roll/BS modifier), **not** +1 to save
     ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far").
   - **Hidden units:** a qualifying infantry/beast/swarm unit fully within a terrain footprint that
     **didn't shoot last turn** counts as **hidden** and can only be shot from within **15"
     detection range**, even with full line of sight ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "HUGE 11th
     Edition Reveals" clarified "within," not "wholly within"). Many army rules grant **+detection
     range** (commonly +3").
   - **Tanks/monsters can nose into terrain and fire out the other side** (line of sight reversed
     from 10th towering behavior) ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").
   - **Plunging fire:** +1 BS when ≥3" up and targeting ground-level units; **Towering** units get
     it vs ground targets within 12" ("EVERY Core Rules Update …").
   - **Heavy:** +1 to hit now allowed after moving **up to 3"** (not just standing still); doesn't
     work while in engagement range ("EVERY Core Rules Update …").
   - **Indirect fire — 3 firing modes:** truly out of LoS to your *whole army* → **hit on 6s only**;
     at least one friendly unit can see the target → **hit on 4+**; the firing weapon sees it → fire
     at normal BS ("Indirect Fire Has CHANGED"; "HUGE Answers …").
   - **Hazardous** is harsher: roll per affected unit/model; on a 1 or 2 → **1 mortal wound** (most
     units) or **3 mortal wounds** for monsters/vehicles ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").
   - **Blast can come in multiples** (Blast 1 / Blast 2 …): +N attacks per 5 models in the target
     unit ("Crushing Impact, Blast Change, Custodes…").
   - **Attacks resolved in pools (big save-sequencing change):** all attacks from one weapon type
     form a pool; attacker chooses pool resolution order; the **defender breaks their unit into
     defensive-stat categories, orders them (characters always last), then applies the lowest save
     rolls first** to the earliest category. This protects high-save characters (a character with
     an invuln in a squad will usually survive a single volley) and lets defenders steer low rolls
     into no-invuln models. **Precision** flips this: the **attacker** orders the categories and may
     place a character earlier — but only works where the character has a distinct defensive profile
     ("MASSIVE 11th Edition Rule Drops from GW Live Streams"; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals" calls this
     "fast dice rolling").
   - **Psychic keyword** now **ignores hit-roll / BS / WS modifiers** (so ignores cover); may or may
     not also ignore wound-roll modifiers (§6) ("FLY Buffed …"; "HUGE Answers …").

4. **Charge phase**
   - **Declare a charge by picking your own unit and rolling**, then see what you can reach — you
     **don't nominate the target first** ("EVERY Core Rules Update …").
   - You need to roll **higher than the distance** to a unit to charge it (with 2" engagement range,
     effectively **~1" harder** than 10th) ("EVERY Core Rules Update …").
   - **Heroic Intervention** moved to **end of the charge phase**; base strat charges up to 12"
     (no CP reroll on long charges); a 2-CP **"Into the Fray"** lets you charge out of sequence vs
     anything within 6" ("EVERY Core Rules Update …").
   - You can **disembark directly into combat** (with a battleshock downside)
     ("EVERY Core Rules Update …").
   - **Disembark places models in base contact / as close as possible to the transport** (not the
     old free 3" projection), shrinking transport threat-range tricks; on a destroyed transport,
     **Deadly Demise now triggers *after* the unit disembarks** (so the unit can be caught in the
     explosion) ("MASSIVE 11th Edition Rule Drops from GW Live Streams").

5. **Fight phase**
   - **Pile-in, Fight, and Consolidation are separated into distinct steps** for the whole phase:
     **all pile-ins first → fight sequence → all consolidations** (both players consolidate after
     all fighting). The active player dictates flow ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "HUGE 11th
     Edition Reveals").
   - **Overrun fight:** a unit that started in combat but is no longer engaged (its enemy died) gets
     a 3"-bonus pile-in and may engage a new unit ("EVERY Core Rules Update …").
   - **Fights First nerfed:** the **charging player picks the first unit to activate** even into a
     Fights-First defender; non-Fights-First step then alternates to the next player who hasn't
     fought ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals" — wording still confusing,
     §6).
   - **Cleave** (new melee rule): like Blast for melee — Cleave N grants +N attacks per 5 models in
     the target, must declare all attacks into that unit ("Cleave Rule Revealed").
   - **Consolidation** onto an objective is now free-form (no central point on terrain-footprint
     objectives) ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").

6. **End of turn**
   - Score primary/secondary as the mission dictates (some at end of command phase, some at end of
     turn, some at end of battle round / end of game — see §3).

### 1.4 End of round / end of game scoring

- **End of battle round** scoring is common for some primaries (e.g. "destroyed ≥1 enemy unit this
  round" → VP; from round 2, "destroyed more enemy than friendly units" → VP)
  ("40k in 40m" Thousand Sons vs Tyranid batrep — Purge the Foe).
- **End of game** scoring exists too (defender-style and final-battle scoring).
- **Game ends after battle round 5.** Final score = primary + secondary; **painting score
  ("with paint")** can add up to ~10 and is reported as a separate total in batreps
  (e.g. "77-65, 87-75 with paint").

## 2. Command Points & Stratagems

- **Generation:** ~**1 CP per battle round** baseline, plus army/datasheet sources (e.g. Gretchin
  farm a CP on a 4+; Zodgrod adds CP). Confirmed in all three batreps.
- **Stratagem count:** roughly **6–9 stratagems** available depending on detachment choices
  ("HUGE Answers …").
- **No stacking stratagems in the same phase on the same unit:** a unit can use multiple
  stratagems in a turn, but you can't, e.g., use a Command Reroll *after* a damage strat on that
  same unit in that phase ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far"). (Practical effect:
  fewer Command Rerolls.)
- **When stratagems fire:** in their relevant phase, including **reactive** strats in the
  opponent's turn (Overwatch at end of enemy movement, Heroic Intervention / Into the Fray at end
  of enemy charge phase, Rapid Ingress, defensive saves/feel-no-pain strats during enemy shooting).
  Batreps show heavy reactive Overwatch use and Command Rerolls.
- **Core stratagem changes** ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "Crushing Impact …"):
  - **Crushing Impact** = renamed Tank Shock, now usable by **monsters and vehicles**, but you
    take **1 mortal wound to yourself per 1 rolled** (bounce-back damage).
  - **Grenades → Explosives** (Explosive keyword).
  - **Rapid Ingress** can't be used turn 1, can't target aircraft.
  - **Desperate Escape** likely replaced/renamed by a **Hazard roll** (1–2 = 1 mortal wound rather
    than losing whole models) ("HUGE Answers …").
  - **Command Reroll** no longer works on desperate-escape-type rolls; can't un-Battleshock an
    already-shocked unit via Insane Bravery (timing) ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").
- **Mission-driven CP economy:** you may **discard an undoable secondary for 1 CP**, and pay **1 CP
  for "New Orders"** to swap a secondary. Both seen repeatedly in batreps.

## 3. Scoring

### 3.1 Primary objectives

- **Per-side primary — force-disposition + matchup driven, NO single game primary:** there is no shared game primary. Each player **selects their own primary mission from the available primary missions**, driven by **their force disposition (detachment) and the matchup** (their force vs the opponent's). The two players' primaries are **asymmetric and paired** — one player's mission is set against the opponent's: e.g. *Unstoppable Force* vs *An Immovable Object*; take-and-hold vs disruption pairings ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals"). They are **independent and may differ**, and **each player scores against their own primary only**.
  - **Data-model consequence:** **each side has its OWN available-primary pool** (separate per side, force-disposition/matchup driven) + its chosen primary from that pool — not a single `match.mission`, and not even a shared pool. Each side's primary VP scores against its own primary.
- **Multiple scoring windows per mission:** 11th primaries commonly have **3–4 ways to score** and
  resolve at **end of command phase**, **end of turn**, and/or **end of battle round / end of
  game** ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals"). This is a deliberate increase
  in mental load vs 10th.
- **Per-turn primary cap pattern (from batreps, mission-dependent):** e.g. *Lynchpin* — from
  battle round 2, at end of command phase, **3 VP per marker** controlled, or **3 VP for the
  deployment-zone marker + 5 VP per other marker, up to 15 VP/turn and 50/game** ("40k in 40m"
  Space Marines batrep). *Purge the Foe* — **4 VP** for destroying ≥1 enemy unit at end of round;
  from round 2, **+4 VP** for destroying more enemy than friendly units; **4 VP** at end of command
  phase for controlling ≥1 objective, **+4 VP** for controlling more than the opponent
  ("40k in 40m" Tyranid batrep).
- **Objectives re-load / re-roll at the start of each battle round** in some missions (which
  markers are "loaded" / "heavily loaded" changes round to round) — batrep 1 (500 Worlds supply
  raid). Attacker vs defender can have **asymmetric scoring** (attacker scores per round, defender
  scores big at end of game) — batrep 1.

### 3.2 Secondary objectives

- **Deck of secondary cards.** You **draw 2 per turn** (~10 over a 5-round game). You may now
  **hold any number in hand** (no longer capped at 2), enabling end-game burst scoring
  ("EVERY Core Rules Update for 11th Edition So Far"). All three batreps show 2-per-turn draws.
- **Fixed vs Tactical:** you may choose **fixed** secondaries (less common, more tournament-y) or
  draw **tactical** secondaries from the deck ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"). Some objectives reward
  tactical over fixed (e.g. Engage on All Fronts pays more for tactical) ("HUGE 11th Edition
  Reveals").
- **VP cap:** **never more than 15 VP from secondaries in a single battle round**
  ("EVERY Core Rules Update …"; "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals").
- **New Orders / discard:** **1 CP** to redraw a secondary; **discard an undoable one for +1 CP**
  (reduces your total draws). Confirmed in batreps and "HUGE 11th Edition Reveals."
- **Secondary card names seen in batreps** (real 11th cards): *Mark for Death / Marked for Death*,
  *Establish Locus*, *Engage on All Fronts*, *No Prisoners*, *Cleanse*, *Recover Assets*,
  *Sabotage*, *Assassination*, *Display of Might*, *Overwhelming Force*, *Secure No Man's Land*,
  *Extend Battle Lines*, *Area Denial*, *Behind Enemy Lines*, *Tempting Target*, *Storm Hostile
  Objective*, *Bring It Down*, *Plunder*, *Outflank*, *Burden of Trust*, *Beacon*, *Center Ground*,
  *Forward Position*, *Recover Assets*. (Some, e.g. *Plunder*, can be returned to the deck if you
  also hold *Cleanse*.) ("HUGE 11th Edition Reveals"; three Play On batreps.)

### 3.3 Final result

Primary VP + Secondary VP, tracked as separate running totals throughout the game; painting score
added at the very end. In the closest batrep, **secondaries decided a tied game on primary**
(both players tied on primary; secondary totals broke the tie) — see batrep 3 finish 77-65.

## 5. Game-Tracker Requirements (grounded in the batreps)

These are the data a competitive player actually records turn-by-turn, derived from watching the
three "40k in 40 minutes" battle reports. The tracker must capture all of the following.

### 5.1 Match-level (set once)

- Two players + factions + **detachment(s)** chosen (and their **force dispositions**).
- **Army lists** per side (units, attached Leader/Support characters, enhancements, wargear,
  points) — note characters are declared as attached at list time.
- **Mission:** the **available-primary pool** + **each side's chosen primary** (per-side, matchup-dependent — NOT one shared primary); plus shared **deployment type** and **terrain layout**.
- **Attacker / defender** roles (some missions score asymmetrically).
- Which units start in **reserve / deep strike / strategic reserve**.
- **Who goes first.**
- Starting **objective markers** and their state (which are "loaded" / "heavily loaded" if the
  mission uses that).

### 5.2 Per battle round (shared, at round start)

- Battle round number (1–5).
- **Objective re-load / re-roll** state for the round (which markers are loaded/heavy this round).
- Any **escalation rule** state (some missions ramp difficulty per round).

### 5.3 Per player-turn / per half-round (the core tracking loop)

For **each player's turn** within a round, the tracker should record:

- **Command points:** CP gained this turn (base + farmed/bonus), CP spent, running CP balance per
  player.
- **Battle Shock:** which units tested, pass/fail, and which are **currently battle-shocked**
  (persistent across turns — needs a per-unit shocked flag/token, not a one-turn flag). *Players
  bring physical tokens for this (Tactical Tortoise "8 Tips").*
- **Secondaries:** the **2 cards drawn** this turn, which were **kept / discarded for CP /
  swapped via New Orders**, and which were **scored** (with VP). Maintain the **hand** (unbounded)
  across turns.
- **Primary scored** this turn / phase (respecting the mission's scoring windows: end of command
  phase, end of turn, end of round) with VP.
- **Per-side running totals: primary VP and secondary VP tracked separately**, plus combined.
- **Stratagems used** (which strat, which phase, CP cost), including **reactive** strats fired
  during the opponent's turn (Overwatch, Heroic Intervention/Into the Fray, defensive saves).
- **Units destroyed / lost** this turn — both "units I destroyed" and "units I lost" (several
  primaries and secondaries — No Prisoners, Bring It Down, Purge the Foe, Display of Might —
  count destroyed units and compare counts). Track **which unit attacked / destroyed which**
  where it matters for scoring.
- **Who is attacking whom** (active player, target units) for combats and key shooting.
- **Board / objective control state** at scoring windows: which player controls each marker, OC
  totals on contested objectives, sticky-objective flags.
- **Persistent effects** that need a token: extended detection range, auspex-scanned / marked
  targets (oath of moment, marked-by-fate), "hidden" status, ongoing buffs. *Tactical Tortoise
  explicitly recommends tokens for detection range, hidden, actions, and battle shock.*
- **Actions** in progress (Plunder, Cleanse, Sabotage, Establish Locus, Recover Assets…) — which
  unit is performing which action and whether it completed. Actions are expected to be a big part
  of 11th.

### 5.4 End of game

- Final **primary VP**, **secondary VP**, and **painting score** per player (reported separately;
  paint can be the tiebreaker-adjacent component, e.g. "with paint" totals).
- Result (win/loss/**draw** — batrep 1 ended 55-55 a true draw; secondaries broke a primary tie in
  batrep 3).

### 5.5 Media

- **Photos** of board state per turn/round are a natural capture point (batreps narrate board state
  every half-turn; competitive players photograph objective/board state for disputes and review).
  Tracker should allow attaching a photo to a round/turn.

## 6. Open Questions / Uncertainty (low-confidence — flag, don't invent)

These were unclear, contradictory, or explicitly unconfirmed in the transcripts (auto-captions are
noisy and these are pre-launch previews):

- **Overwatch timing vs the rules text.** Creators say Overwatch fires at **end of movement phase**
