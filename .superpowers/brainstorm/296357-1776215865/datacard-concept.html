Key Design Elements

  • GW stat bar format — M/T/SV/W/LD/OC/INV in a horizontal strip
  • Weapon tables — ranged and melee separated, weapon abilities highlighted in amber
  • Abilities section — green-themed cards with full rules text
  • Context overlay — red semi-transparent highlight showing WHY this unit matters for the current query
  • Faction header — blue gradient with unit name, keywords, points
